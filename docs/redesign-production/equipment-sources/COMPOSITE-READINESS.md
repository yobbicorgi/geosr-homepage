## USV source distinction

The three USV files are different visual assets, not three views of one verified model: `usv-catamaran-page-original.gif` is a stylized yellow-catamaran illustration; `usv-usv20s-page-original.png` is a yellow twin-hull craft on a wooded waterway; `usv-usvcom-page-original.png` is a red/black craft in open water. The page places them in the “무인선 이용 관측” business capability section. Its copy describes unmanned-vessel observation and survey capabilities across marine, river and lake settings, but does not provide a dated, located operation report or map each image to a verified model/specification. Treat them as page illustrations/examples, not evidence that these three assets depict the same craft or a specific field deployment. `USV20S.png` is a filename, not sufficient proof of a model claim.

## Production guidance

- Equipment-detail entries for Hanuri, FireFly6, BlueROV2 and RBR are catalog material. The USV page is a capability/service description, not a documented field case.
- The small Hanuri, FireFly6, RBR and BlueROV2 page thumbnails are shape references only. Do not scale them up to fill a 2560×1440 film frame; request high-resolution cleared originals or make a new photograph with verified equipment.
- For a single scene with Hanuri and a USV, commission or source one real matched capture showing both craft. Compositing the existing vessel images would mix incompatible backgrounds, viewpoints, lighting and scale.
- Do not identify the RBR thumbnail as a confirmed Solo-TU: the detail-page title and source filename disagree. Resolve the exact unit before depicting it in a field installation.
- This archive provides no actual buoy/mooring photo or verified TPRBM image. Keep those sources marked missing; patent-document thumbnails are not equipment photographs.
